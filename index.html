hey, I'm Stash 👋

I'm an Information Technology undergraduate in Mumbai who likes owning a product from the first sketch to the last deploy. Right now I design websites and product interfaces in Figma for clients across the USA, UAE and beyond — and because I also write the code, my designs stay practical to build.
